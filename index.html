Our Story

Celebrating 20 Years of God’s Faithfulness

For two decades, CSF Church has been a house of worship, refuge, and family for generations of believers. What began as a small gathering of faithful hearts has grown into a thriving community bound together by grace — and every step of the journey has borne witness to God’s unfailing faithfulness.

01

Our Journey

From humble beginnings in a rented hall to a beloved sanctuary of our own, our journey has been shaped by prayer, perseverance, and the unwavering hand of God guiding every season.

02

Our Mission

To make disciples of Christ, nurture faith through worship and fellowship, and serve our community with the same love and compassion we have received.

03

Our Vision

To raise a generation rooted in faith, marked by hope, moved by love, and devoted to service — carrying the Gospel forward for the next twenty years and beyond.

A Word From Our Shepherd

Message from the Pastor

“Twenty years ago, a small band of believers stepped out in faith, not knowing all that lay ahead — only trusting the One who called us. Today, as we look back across two decades of worship, trial, growth, and grace, my heart overflows with gratitude. This anniversary is not a monument to what we have built, but a testimony to what God has done through an imperfect people who simply said yes to Him. Thank you for being part of this family. Let us step into the next twenty years with the same faith that carried us through the first.”

Rev. Dr. Samuel Okonkwo-Reyes
Senior Pastor, CSF Church

Two Decades in Review

Church Memories

A look back at the milestones that shaped our first twenty years.

  1. Year 1 · 2006

    A Humble Beginning

    CSF Church holds its first service in a rented community hall with just 32 members gathered in faith.

  2. Year 5 · 2011

    Our First Home

    The congregation moves into its first permanent building after years of prayer and sacrificial giving.

  3. Year 8 · 2014

    Growing Ministries

    Youth, children's, and outreach ministries are launched, expanding the church's reach across the community.

  4. Year 10 · 2016

    A Decade of Grace

    The 10th anniversary is celebrated with over 400 members — ten times the founding congregation.

  5. Year 14 · 2020

    Faith Through Trial

    The church family perseveres through a season of global uncertainty, sustaining online worship and care for one another.

  6. Year 17 · 2023

    New Sanctuary Built

    A new, larger sanctuary is dedicated, built entirely through the generosity of the church family.

  7. Year 20 · 2026

    Twenty Years of Faithfulness

    We gather to celebrate two decades of Faith, Hope, Love, and Service — and look ahead to all that is still to come.
